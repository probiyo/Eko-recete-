3. Data Collection

Spectrophotometer wavelength selection is optimized for chlorophyll a and 663 nm absorption values are recorded. pH measurements are made with precision electrode pH meters or alternative extraction protocols.

4. Data Analysis and EU Green Deal Live Mechanism

Obtained optical density and pH deviation data are transformed into chemical input optimization within the framework of the Farm to Fork strategy.

🇪🇺 EU Green Deal & Farm to Fork (Tarladan Sofraya) Canlı Optimizasyon Mekanizması:

  • Stres Skoru Hesaplama Formülü:
    Stress_Score = w₁ · |pH_optimum - pH_measured| + w₂ · (A_reference - A_sample)

    (Combining pH deviation and chlorophyll absorption drop with weighted coefficients to convert the plant's physiological stress level into a numerical value.)

  • Kimyasal Girdi Optimizasyonu ve Gübre Tasarrufu (%22.4):
    Saving_%_fertilizers = (1 - (Input_optimize / Input_traditional)) · 100

    (The ratio preventing over-fertilization based on the effect of ion/pH dynamics on chlorophyll synthesis according to Campbell Biology and Guyton & Hall principles.)

  • Karbon Ayak İzi Düşüşü (142 kg CO₂ eşdeğeri):
    CO₂_Reduction = Σ (Input_reduced · EF_chemical)

    (Annual savings per decare resulting from the product of the amount of chemical reduced through the precision agriculture regime and the emission factor.)

  • Pestisit Optimizasyonu (%30):
    Pest_Opt = (Threshold_standard / Alert_early_stage) · 30%

    (Threshold value matrix where unnecessary chemical spraying is prevented thanks to early warning algorithms.)
